Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Search and radio

Find music and videos. Any result can start a radio of similar songs.

Your library

Liked songs, history and playlists, ordered the way you want.

Offline listening

Songs you play in full stay available without a connection.

Sleep timer

Stop after a set time, or at the end of the current song.

Self-updating

Music sources are signed plugins that update on their own, without a new app.

Android and Linux

The same features on both, each designed to feel at home.

Privacy

Private by design.

No account and no tracking. Your library stays on your device. Plugins run in a sandbox, can reach only the sites they declare, and never your files.
